StretchText for Pollinations

Turn any web page into stretchtext. Hover a paragraph, hit + stretch, and a Pollinations text model grows the passage in place, tinted so you can see what is grown. Hit shrink and the page is exactly what it was. Grown text stretches one level deeper still.

Ted Nelson described stretchtext in 1967. Writing every level by hand was too much work, so it never caught on. Generated text makes it cheap.

Download stretchtext.zip

Install (load unpacked)

  1. Download and unzip stretchtext.zip.
  2. Open chrome://extensions in a Chromium browser.
  3. Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
  5. Open the extension's options and connect your Pollen.

Readers pay with their own Pollen: connect with the BYOP device flow in options (approve in your browser, the extension polls for your key), or paste your own Pollinations API key. Keys stay in chrome.storage.local on your machine. Every call goes to gen.pollinations.ai with your key.
